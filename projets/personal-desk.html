Personal Desk

A local workspace for emails, your calendar and tasks, with notes for your ideas and an encrypted vault for copies of documents.

  • SQLite
  • Microsoft Graph

I had a few ideas in mind: a dashboard for my emails and calendar, somewhere to keep my notes, and eventually a “second brain”. Personal Desk grew out of wanting to bring those ideas into one application.

It brings together Microsoft emails, calendar events and tasks, alongside searchable notes. I also added several themes and a vault for encrypted copies of documents. A version with a Windows installer is available.

The app works locally, with optional Claude planning after reviewing the data to send. Notes and tasks can be exported and restored. For now, encryption applies only to document copies placed in the vault, not to ordinary notes. The vault has not been independently audited.
